What is Food Drive

A food drive is an organized event or campaign to colect food donations from people.Groups like schools, office, or charities run these drives to gather items for people who do not have enough to eat.

Why I choose to volunteer

Choosing to volunteer for a food drive is a powerful way to make a direct, tangible impact on local hunger while boosting your own personal growth and well-being.
