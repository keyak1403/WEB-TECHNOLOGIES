ABOUT CYBER SECURITY

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks.
These cyberattacks are usually aimed at accessing information; extorting money from users through ransomware; or interrupting normal business processes.

CORE CONCEPTS:-

  • Confidentiality: Keeps sensitive data private and hidden from unauthorized users.
  • Integrity: Ensure data remains accurate, complete, and free from unauthorized tampering.
  • Availability: Guarantees that systems and data are ready and accessible for authorized users when needed.
